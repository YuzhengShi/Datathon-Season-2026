# Demo guide

Everything runs on one laptop, with no internet: `.\scripts\start_app.ps1` (or `python -m navigator.cli serve --mode live`, then open
<http://127.0.0.1:8000/>). If the screen is small, zoom the browser to 110-125%. `-Port 8080` if Windows refuses the port.

## Three minutes

1. **The problem (20 s).** Indigenous students are told "there is funding", but it is spread over dozens of websites with different deadlines, and some
   of what is listed is years old or no longer offered.
2. **Answer a few questions (40 s).** Start, choose *First Nations*, *registered: yes*, *British Columbia*, *University / full-time*, *Simon Fraser University*.
   Point out that every question has a Skip and that nothing is saved.
3. **The store page (60 s).** Search "nurse", then clear it; sort by *Amount*; open the *Source* filter and show the two kinds of card:
   *Checked on the provider's page* and *Government directory entry* (older, no deadline). Save two awards to **My list**.
   *Optional (30 s):* go back with *Edit my answers* and leave *registered under the Indian Act* unanswered. The federal PSSSP card now says *More answers
   would help*, and its page asks exactly that question and says that no card number is needed.
4. **One award in depth (40 s).** Open a Vancouver Island University award (browse everything, search "Laura Finch"): "One application covers 24 other
   awards", the deadline "every year on Apr 30", and the provider's own words with a link and the date checked.
5. **Honesty (20 s).** Mention that the *First Citizens Fund* bursary is in our data as archived (its provider paused it, so students never see it), and say
   what the site never claims: it does not predict who wins, and "Meets what we can check" is not "eligible".

## Questions you will get

* **Why not search the web?** The page says which source each fact came from and when it was last checked; directory entries are labelled; paused
  programs are hidden; deadlines say when no time zone is given.
* **Is the data right?** Every quote on a detail page is checked automatically against the stored page. A person has *not* reviewed the records yet; the site says so.
* **What about UBC?** Its pages refuse automated access (HTTP 403). We did not work around that; we are asking UBC for permission, and the system can import
  a page that a person saves in a browser.
* **Privacy?** No name, no status number, no documents; answers stay in the open tab; no cookies or tracking; the service neither stores nor logs the profile.
* **Who pays / who runs it?** Free and non-commercial; no ads.

## Numbers you can quote (from the database, 8 October 2026)

59 records, 58 shown to students (one is archived because its provider paused it): 31 read on a provider's own page (25 of them Vancouver Island University
awards), 4 official program pages, 8 from SFU's awards table and 15 from a federal directory page that can be many years old (the oldest was last modified in 2012).
